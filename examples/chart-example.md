# Chart

Stack: React 19 + Vite, Tailwind, framer-motion
Platforms: web (desktop and mobile browser)
Visits: on

## Top tasks
1. See today's orders and what is late, from the home screen.
2. Export the day's orders, from the home screen.

## Users and context
Bakery managers, on their feet, phone in one hand, between 6am and 11am. Gloves on. Bright room.

## Brand
- Color: `#1F4E3D` (shopfront green), from the painted sign; used for the mark and primary actions only.
- Type: system UI for everything; the order numbers use tabular figures.
- Keep: the green mark and the paper-like background. The owner chose them and they appear on the packaging.

## Accepted
- `ui.status-dot`: the green dot marks an order paid in cash and is paired with the word "cash". Accepted by the owner on 2026-11-18.
- Dense list rows: this is a tool used every ten minutes, not a landing page.

## Off limits
- Checkout flow: owned by the payments team.
- The printed receipt layout: generated by the printer firmware.
