# Manifesto

1. **Slop is generic, not ugly.** The problem is a screen that could belong to anyone, not a screen that is unattractive.
2. **Friction outranks aesthetics.** A missing back button is a bigger design failure than a purple gradient.
3. **Truth outranks polish.** A beautiful screen that invents a number is broken.
4. **The platform is the design system.** Native controls, native navigation, native text scaling. Bespoke is a debt unless it carries the brand.
5. **Content first.** The thing people came for gets the space. Greetings, chrome, and section labels get what is left.
6. **Deterministic evidence beats opinion.** A finding names a file, a line, a rule, a fix. "I do not like it" is not a finding.
7. **A small diff beats a redesign.** Minimum effective change. Healthy screens get told they are healthy.
8. **One template is not better than another.** Purple gradients and calm white cards are the same failure with different lighting.
9. **The evidence has limits.** Performance is not visible in code. Screens are not visible without running the app. Say what was not checked.
10. **Local first.** Source, screenshots, and design decisions do not leave the machine. No account, no telemetry, no cloud.
11. **The screen is the patient.** Critique the artifact, not the person, and never guess who or what wrote it.
12. **Pass your own test.** If the tool's own interface is sloppy, the tool is not finished.
